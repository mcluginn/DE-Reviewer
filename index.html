01 · Notation & Setup

Core Notation & Differential Form

Differential form $M(x,y)\\,dx + N(x,y)\\,dy = 0$, explicit form $dy/dx = F(x,y)$, and level curve solutions $\Psi(x,y) = C$.

02 · Exact Equations

Exactness Test & Partial Derivatives

Testing equality of mixed partial derivatives $\frac{\partial M}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial N}{\partial x}$ and avoiding student traps.

Review Topic →
03 · Exact Equations

Solving Exact Differential Equations

Integrating $M$ to obtain $\Psi = \int M\\,dx + g(y)$, differentiating with respect to $y$, and matching $N$ to find $g(y)$.

Review Topic →
04 · Homogeneity

Degree of Homogeneous Functions

Evaluating total term degree $a + b$ in $x^a y^b$ and verifying the scaling condition $f(tx, ty) = t^n f(x,y)$.

Review Topic →
05 · Linear Equations

Linear First-Order Differential Equations

Canonical standard form $y' + P(x)y = Q(x)$, integrating factor $\mu(x) = e^{\int P(x)\\,dx}$, and product rule integration.

Review Topic →
06 · Separation of Variables

Separable Differential Equations

Isolating differentials $\frac{1}{h(y)}\\,dy = g(x)\\,dx$, antiderivative integration, and solving explicitly for $y(x)$.

Review Topic →
07 · Homogeneous DEs

Homogeneous First-Order DEs

Ratio substitution $y = vx$, product rule derivative $y' = v + x v'$, and separating variables in $v$ and $x$.

Review Topic →
08 · Boundary Conditions

Initial-Value Problems (IVPs)

Obtaining the family general solution first, then substituting $(x_0, y_0)$ to determine the unique integration constant $C$.

Review Topic →
09 · Orthogonality

Orthogonal Trajectories

Eliminating family constant $c$, applying negative reciprocal slope $m_\perp = -1/m$, and integrating orthogonal curves.

Review Topic →
10 · Transcendental Forms

Exact Equations with Trigonometric Terms

Exactness involving multi-variable products $y\cos(xy)$, chain rule differentiation, and $\tan y$ potential components.

Review Topic →
11 · Special Factors

Integrating Factors for Non-Exact Equations

Testing single-variable conditions $\frac{M_y - N_x}{N} = f(x)$ and $\frac{N_x - M_y}{M} = g(y)$ to convert to exact forms.

Review Topic →

🧭 Compact Decision Protocol (6-Step Classification)

When solving exam questions: (1) Check exactness $M_y = N_x$, (2) Check linear form $y' + Py = Q$, (3) Try separating variables $\frac{dy}{h(y)} = g(x)dx$, (4) If $y/x$ ratio, substitute $y = vx$, (5) For orthogonal trajectories use $m_\perp = -1/m$, (6) If not exact, test $\mu(x)$ and $\mu(y)$ criteria.
